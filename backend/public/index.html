Before You Begin

A few things to know before you start the assessment.

What to expect: This assessment has 53 short statements and questions about your day-to-day experience as a teacher — your energy, focus, sleep, sense of achievement, relationships at work, and how work and personal life currently fit together. It takes most people 12–15 minutes to complete.

How to answer: For each question, choose the single option that best reflects how you've genuinely been feeling recently — not how you think you should feel, and not how you'd like things to be. There are no right or wrong answers; the value of this assessment depends entirely on how honestly you answer.

Your answers are saved as you go. You can move through the questions at your own pace, and before you finally submit, you'll get a chance to review everything and change any answer you're not sure about.

Once submitted, your responses are locked and your report is generated from them. If you ever need to retake the assessment after that, please write to us at navasetuinitiatives@gmail.com or call 8556840001, and we'll enable it for you.

This is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical or diagnostic test. If you're going through something difficult right now, please also reach out to someone you trust or a mental health professional — this assessment is a starting point, not a substitute for that support.
